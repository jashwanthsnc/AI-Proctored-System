Real-time Submission: Auto-save and instant submission

Behavior Analysis: Monitors suspicious activities and patterns 
Browser Security: Fullscreen enforcement, tab switch detection, copy-paste blocking

Exam Management: Create, edit, schedule, and publish exams 
Violation Tracking: View flagged incidents with timestamp and evidence 
Analytics & Reports: Performance statistics and cheating analytics
